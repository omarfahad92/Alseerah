# Encyclopedia content

Full stories and hadith insights live here, outside `index.html`, and load only when a reader opens them.
Deploy this folder next to `index.html` (GitHub Pages, or a local copy opened by double-click; both work).

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `sources.js` | The registry of books and graders every citation refers to, and the 114 surahs (edit by hand) |
| `stories/<id>.js` | One full story |
| `stories/<id>.draft.js` | A new version of an approved story, in review (visitors keep seeing the approved one) |
| `insights/<section>.js` | Explanations for the hadith boxes of one page (e.g. `shamail`) |
| `manifest.js` | Generated by `node tools/enc.js build`. Never edit by hand |

Each file is plain JSON wrapped in one call, `Enc.register('<path>', { ... });`, so it loads from `file://` too.

## Writing a story: the documented narrative (`"format": "narrative"`)

A story reads as one connected narrative, with its explanations kept visibly apart from it:

| Block | Use it for | Must include |
|---|---|---|
| `prose` | The story itself: plain, literary Arabic that links the events | At least one source cited inline (see below). `"lead": true` on the opening paragraph |
| `hadith` | A report quoted in full (or with its omissions marked "…"), shown as a pull-quote in the flow | `ref` such as `bukhari:3231`, `grade` (`sahih`/`hasan`), and `grader` (`albani`) outside al-Bukhari and Muslim; `also` for the same hadith elsewhere; `"full": true` to fold the complete text under it (see below) |
| `quran` | A whole verse or passage, shown centred with its surah and ayah | `ref` such as `quran:9:40` or `quran:46:29-31`, and `surah` (ar/en) |
| `reflect` | A short "وقفة": the meaning or lesson, kept apart from the history | At least one verse or hadith cited inline |
| `aside` | An explanation box: `kind` `place` (the setting), `people` (who is who), `words` (the meanings of a passage) or `riwaya` (how a report reached us, variant wordings, the commentators' corrections) | A `title` (ar/en) and at least one source cited inline; each line of the text (`\n`) is a paragraph, the same number of lines in both languages |

Inline marks inside `prose`, `reflect`, `aside` and `lessons` (the Arabic and the English must cite the same sources):

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `[«…»](h:bukhari:3883)` | Words quoted verbatim from a hadith; a numbered note is added after them |
| `[﴿…﴾](q:21:107)` | A quoted verse (surah:ayah, checked against the surah list) |
| `[^umari]`, `[^bukhari:3896]` | A source note for the sentence it follows (Seerah books such as `umari` and `zad`, or a hadith) |
| `[text](g:term)` | A glossary term, defined in `glossary` |
| `[Abu Bakr](p:c_abubakr)` | A Companion: the name opens a small popup with his card (the key of his card in index.html) |

A hadith quoted inline from outside al-Bukhari and Muslim needs its grade in the story's `grades`, for example
`"grades": { "abudawud:5156": { "grade": "sahih", "grader": "albani" } }`. Each scene may have a `sub` line (place and time).

Scholarly rules: every historical sentence carries a source; direct speech is quoted only in the words of an authentic
report; no feelings or intentions the reports do not state; where the authentic reports are silent, say so, and put the
popular but unestablished details in `unproven` (grade `weak`, `mursal` or `fabricated`, with a note). They are shown in a
collapsed box, never inside the story.

The older block layout (`context`, `narration`) still works for stories that do not set `format`.

## The depth standard (the model: `stories/jahr.js`)

Approved by the site owner on 2026-09-29. A full story is a reference the reader can live in:

- **Length follows the sound material.** About 5–8 minutes for an ordinary event and 8–10 for a major one (the site counts
  150 words a minute, so 1000–1500 words). Never pad: every paragraph adds a text, a sourced explanation or a lesson, and
  where the authentic reports are few the story is simply shorter.
- **Gather the authentic wordings** of the event from the collections (e.g. al-Bukhari with Muslim, and a longer wording
  of the same chain elsewhere) and weave them together. Quote short hadiths and verses in full; in a long one, mark each
  omission with "…". For a very long hadith, give its complete text too (below).
- **Keep the layers apart** so the reader always knows what is text, history, explanation or lesson:

| Layer | Where |
|---|---|
| The story | `prose`, every factual sentence cited |
| The texts | `hadith` and `quran` blocks, and inline quotes |
| The explanations | `aside` boxes (`place`, `people`, `words`, `riwaya`) |
| The rare words | `glossary`: each entry reads `"term: meaning"` in both languages, with `ref` naming the book the meaning comes from (e.g. `nawawi:3/82`); `"wordlists": true` lists a scene's words after it |
| The lessons | `lessons`, each with a `kind` — `aqidah`, `dawah`, `tarbiyah` or `qiyadah` — and citing or quoting the text it rests on |

- **Places, people and feelings come only from sources.** An authentic small detail (e.g. «فجعل يطلب الفيء يمشي فيه من
  شدة حرِّ الرمضاء») carries a scene better than any description of our own.

## The complete text of a long hadith

When a story quotes only parts of a very long hadith (e.g. the emigration hadith, `bukhari:3905`), the reader can open
the whole of it under the excerpt with the «النص الكامل للحديث» button, which also shows its reading time. The complete
text is optional reading: it is not counted in the story's minutes.

```json
"fulltext": {
  "bukhari:3905": {
    "narrator": { "ar": "عن عائشة (رضي الله عنها)", "en": "From 'A'ishah (رضي الله عنها)" },
    "ar": "first paragraph\nsecond paragraph …",
    "en": "the same paragraphs in English"
  }
}
```

and `"full": true` on the `hadith` block (usually the first) that quotes from it. The text is the matn without its chain,
copied from a vowelled edition, plain (no inline marks), in paragraphs cut at the natural breaks of the report, the same
number in both languages. `tools/enc.js check` compares the letters of every stretch of the excerpt between "…" marks
with the complete text (ignoring vowel marks and punctuation) and reports any stretch that is not there word for word.

## Books and locators

| Source kind | Cited as | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| `hadith` | By number | `bukhari:3905`, `ahmad:14456` (al-Risalah numbering) |
| `sharh` (commentaries, tafsir) | By volume/page | `fath:8/502`, `nawawi:4/167`; `ibnkathir` without a page |
| `book` (reference works) | By volume/page | `yaqut:4/142`; `tarikh` by its entry |
| `sirah` | Whole book, or a one-volume book by page | `umari`, `alali:116` |

A report graded by a compiler rather than by the standard graders (e.g. Ibrahim al-Ali's «صحيح السيرة») is quoted as plain
«…» with a note, and its grading is stated in a `riwaya` box. The citation popup shows "الجزء والصفحة" or "الصفحة" for books.

## Its card and its links

| Field | Use |
|---|---|
| `topics` | One or two of the six topics in `journeyData.topics`: `dawah`, `ghazawat`, `mujizat`, `ibadah`, `akhlaq`, `bayt` |
| `people` | The Companions who take part, by the key of their card (`c_abubakr`, …): the reader lists them, and each Companion's card lists the stories he appears in |
| `places` | Map places (`mecca`, `madinah`, …) besides `place.map`: the map panel lists the stories set there |
| `icon`, `tone` | The card's icon (a sprite name) and colour; required when the story has no summary card |

## Order, the journey and "next story"

The stories page is drawn from `journeyData` in `index.html`: the eras in chronological order, then his lasting guidance by
theme. An item is either a summary card (`"card"`, a modal key) or a full story (`"story"`, an id in this folder) that opens
straight in the reader, its card drawn from the story file. `journeyData` already holds a place for every planned story; a
new story appears in its place as soon as its file exists and is published (or with `?preview` while it is a draft). A story
without a summary needs no `modal`. `check` makes sure every story has its place. The reader's "next story" follows the same
order, so a story file does not need a `next` field.

## No emoji

Nothing in the content or on the page uses colour emoji; `check` rejects them. Icons are the line icons of the sprite in
`index.html` (`ico('name')` in scripts).

## Digits

Arabic text is written with Arabic-Indic digits (١٢٣), English with Western digits; `check` rejects Western digits in any
Arabic field (the references inside inline marks, such as `h:bukhari:3906`, are not text and may use them).

## «صحِّح معلومتك» and the Mothers of the Believers

- Every story's `unproven` notes are copied into the manifest; the «صحِّح معلومتك» page (`#myths`, one report: `#myths/<story>.<id>`)
  lists them with the boxes found inside the summary and Companion cards, so a new note appears there by itself.
- The wives' timeline and cards are drawn from `const wivesTimeline = {…JSON…};` in `index.html`: each wife's dates (with their
  source), one authenticated report (with its grade outside al-Bukhari and Muslim) and her stories. `check` validates it.

## The map and story routes

- The map is drawn from real coordinates (north up, the Hejaz magnified, distances not to scale); a new place needs a pin in
  `index.html`, its entry in `mapData`, and its coordinates in `MAP_XY` (the build's `t12/geo.js` computes them).
- A story can be drawn as a route: add it to `const mapRoutes = {…JSON…};` in `index.html` — its stations in order, each
  `{ "at": <map place>, "scene": <scene id>, "note": {ar, en, ref}? , "bend": number? }`. `check` makes sure every place and
  scene exists. The route's places are listed in the reader and on the map panels automatically.
- Any scene can be linked: `#story/<id>/<scene>`.

## Workflow

1. Write or edit a file, then run `node tools/enc.js check` and `node tools/enc.js build`.
2. New content is `draft`: it stays hidden. Open the site with `?preview` to see drafts (a badge shows preview mode; it has an Exit button).
   To rewrite an approved story, save the new text as `stories/<id>.draft.js` (registering `stories/<id>.draft`): `?preview` shows it, visitors keep the approved text.
3. Export the citations for the scholarly audit: `node tools/enc.js audit audit.csv`.
4. When the audit passes: `node tools/enc.js approve stories/taif --by "Reviewer"` (or `insights/shamail`, `insights/shamail#<key>`, or `all`).
   The item goes live; a new version takes the approved one's place. `node tools/enc.js draft <target>` takes it back.
